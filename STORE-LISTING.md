## Full description — Play, max 4000 characters

```
Dr. Joshi's is the patient app of Dr Joshi's HHRC — your own clinic record, in your pocket.

Everything your doctor records during a visit is here, written for you to read, along with the
medicines you are on and the reminders that help you take them on time.

YOUR MEDICAL RECORD
• Every visit: vitals, diagnosis, your doctor's notes, and the treatment plan
• Your Ayurveda assessment, as your doctor recorded it
• Lab results over time, so you can see what is improving
• Prescriptions you can open and show at any pharmacy
• Tests that are due, before someone has to phone you about them

MEDICINES AND REMINDERS
• Your current medicines with the dose and timing
• Reminders at 8 AM, 2 PM and 8 PM — tick each dose as you take it
• Request a refill without calling the clinic
• Add medicines prescribed by other doctors, so your record is complete

APPOINTMENTS
• Book a consultation or therapy session
• See what is coming up, and your past visits
• Cancel, or request a different time
• Check in before your visit by answering a few questions about how you are feeling

TALK TO YOUR CLINIC
• Message the clinic and get a reply from the staff who know your case
• Share a photograph of a lab report done elsewhere and have it added to your record
• Give feedback after a visit

FAMILY
• Hold more than one person's records on one phone — a parent, a child
• Give a family member access to yours, and take it back whenever you want

YOUR PRIVACY
Your records are yours. They are never sold and never shared with advertisers. Everything travels
encrypted, and only you and your treating clinic can see it. You can ask us to delete your account
and data at any time, from inside the app.

Dr. Joshi's does not diagnose you and does not replace your doctor. Everything clinical here is
written by the doctors treating you at the clinic.

In an emergency, call your doctor or go to a hospital — do not use this app.
```
*(about 1,850 characters)*
